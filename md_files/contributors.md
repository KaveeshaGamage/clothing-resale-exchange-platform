# Project Contributors, Roles, and Functionalities

This document describes the team roles, contribution areas, and platform functionalities each contributor works on for the Clothing Resale & Exchange Platform.

## Team Roles and Functionalities

| Contributor | Role | Contribution area | Functionalities and responsibilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| KP Gamge (Me) | Software Developer | Backend | Develops and maintains backend functionality for user registration, login and authentication; product listing creation and management; and order creation, cancellation, and order history. |
| SD Senevirathne | Scrum Master | Backend | Contributes to backend functionality for retail product verification and listing approval or rejection, delivery-status updates, and administration workflows. Coordinates Scrum ceremonies, tracks progress, and helps remove blockers. |
| SAKG Samarathunga | Product Owner | Frontend | Contributes to customer-facing frontend functionality, including browsing and searching listings, viewing product details, managing the cart and wishlist, selling items, and viewing orders. Defines and prioritizes requirements and acceptance expectations for product features. |
| TDVN Silva | UI/UX Designer | Frontend and UI/UX design | Designs and contributes to user interfaces for customer, retail, and administrator workflows, including clear navigation, responsive layouts, accessible interaction patterns, and consistent visual presentation across forms, listings, dashboards, and order or delivery views. |

## Contribution Areas

### Backend

- **KP Gamge (Me):** User authentication and account workflows; product listing CRUD and availability; order processing and seller order history.
- **SD Senevirathne:** Retail verification and approval workflows; delivery management and status updates; supporting administrative workflows and backend coordination.

### Frontend

- **SAKG Samarathunga:** Customer marketplace and shopping workflows, including shop/listing views, selling items, cart, wishlist, and order views; product requirements and prioritization.
- **TDVN Silva:** UI/UX design and frontend presentation for customer, retail, and admin experiences, including responsive styling, navigation, forms, dashboards, and status feedback.

## Collaboration

The contributors coordinate across backend, frontend, and design work so that the platform's customer shopping and selling journeys connect with retail verification, order processing, and delivery workflows. The Product Owner prioritizes product needs, while the Scrum Master supports planning and team progress.
